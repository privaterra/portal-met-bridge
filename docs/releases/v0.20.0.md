**portal-met-bridge 0.20.0 — alpha.** Meticulous & Belka Portal conductivity data visualization & analysis: a local web app that records a [Belka Portal](https://www.belka.coffee/)'s conductivity stream alongside a [Meticulous](https://meticuloushome.com/) espresso shot, integrates it against the cup weight, estimates TDS from your own refractometer readings, reviews shots with an AI model of your choice, hands the brew to Beanconqueror, and shows a machine dashboard. Runs on your Mac (or a Raspberry Pi with Home Assistant); nothing is installed on the machine or the Portal.

**Settings you can fold, reviews that know what other owners do, and a maintenance schedule.** Everything in [0.19.1](https://github.com/privaterra/portal-met-bridge/releases/tag/v0.19.1) still holds — including that the Portal driver has not yet been proven on the author's own Portal — plus the changes below.

## What changed since 0.19.1

- **Settings sections fold.** Settings opens *compact*: the section names only, each opening with a click. A picker at the top chooses how they stand when Settings opens — **compact**, **detailed** (all open, every option listed) or **as I left them** — next to *Expand all* / *Collapse all*. Links such as *Settings → Files and folders* open their section.
- **Meticulous Community in the AI review** (Settings → AI review, on by default). [Meticulous Community](https://community.meticuloushome.com) is where owners log and rate their brews; a review now sets your dose, ratio, temperature and time beside what owners rate highest for the same coffee, roaster, origin and process, and grinder, in a new *Community comparison* section with links back. A checklist says what to read — *Coffee*, *Roaster*, *Grinder*, *Brews by origin and process*, *Pour-over brews*, *Brew profiles* — all on by default. How it reaches the model depends on the provider:
  - **Claude (Anthropic)**: the request carries the *Meticulous Community (Coffee)* connector and Claude looks things up itself;
  - **ChatGPT (OpenAI)**: the query starts with `@Meticulous Community`, and the app looks the answers up and adds them;
  - **Grok** and **Gemini**: the query mentions *Meticulous Community* and carries the answers the app looked up.

  Only names are asked about (coffee, roaster, grinder, origin and process), never your shots. A third mode only *names* Community, for a query you copy into the Claude, ChatGPT or Grok app where the connector is added.
- **What the review contains** (Settings → AI review): a checklist of the report's sections (the existing ones on), a checklist of what is sent with each shot (the brewed profile, the per-second trace, your rating), and **Show the query before it is sent** — *Review with AI* opens a text window with the prepared query to edit before sending. *Edit query…* on the Analysis tab opens it any time; *Copy query* takes it elsewhere.
- **Grok (xAI)** joins Claude, OpenAI and Gemini as a provider.
- **New section: Heating** (after Machine). *Ready to brew* moved here from Machine. New: **Save the heating graph and its data** (off by default) — the Machine tab's *Temperatures and power* gets *Graph (JPEG)*, *Report (PDF)* and *Data (CSV)* buttons; the files download through the browser with the brew they belong to (profile and its temperature, machine state, the ready-to-brew rule, the coffee, the last brew), and the graph keeps 30 minutes instead of five.
- **New section: Brew Prep.** (after AI review). *Pick-lists* moved here. New: **Grind Prep** — RDT, WDT, distribution tool, filter screen (off), tamper, single-dose grinder — shared with the AI review; and **Water** — pick or type the water you brew with, *use by default* to include it in every review and note it on each new shot.
- **Cleaning & maintenance** (Machine tab; switch under Settings → Machine). The schedule Meticulous publishes under [Maintenance and Repairs](https://meticuloushome.com/pages/maintenance-and-repairs): the weekly internal cleaning, the monthly chamber cleaning and the three-monthly deep cleaning (not the routine cleaning after every shot and daily). Enter the date and time each was done; the panel and the left rail remind you of the next one — on schedule, due soon, overdue — and count the brews since. Under it, **Piston**: after every brew the app says whether the piston travelled and came back to rest and the machine returned to idle — *all operating OK* — or what it saw instead.
- **Analysis → Shots in this review**: a ticked shot has **Edit** and **Delete** buttons in the list.

## You may notice

- **Settings looks empty at first**: it is compact. Click a section name, or choose *detailed* at the top to have it open the old way.
- **A review now talks to `community.meticuloushome.com`** (from your browser, or from Anthropic's servers with Claude). Untick *Use Meticulous Community data* under Settings → AI review to stop that. If Community cannot be reached the review says so and carries on.
- **The Grok provider and Claude's Community connector have not been run against the live services by the author** — they are covered by the unit tests and a mock. If Claude's connector is refused, the app looks the answers up itself and sends the review again. Reports welcome.
- **The piston check is cautious**: it reads only what the machine reports, in relative terms. Take *all operating OK* as "nothing unusual was reported".
- The cleaning dates live with the settings; backups and settings exports carry them.

## Install

**macOS — the app (no terminal).** Download `portal-met-bridge-0.20.0-macos.zip` (or the `.dmg`), move *Portal Met Bridge.app* to Applications (replace the old one) and open it. Your shots, beans, reviews and settings are kept: they live in the browser and in `~/Documents/portal-met-bridge`. It runs on Apple silicon and Intel, starts its own small server on `http://localhost:8787` and opens Chrome. It is not signed: the first time, right-click → Open.

**Home Assistant on a Raspberry Pi 5 (or any Home Assistant machine).** Settings → Apps → Portal Met Bridge → **Update** (or add the repository `https://github.com/privaterra/portal-met-bridge` and install). The image is rebuilt on the device with current Alpine packages.

**From source.**

```sh
git pull               # or: git clone https://github.com/privaterra/portal-met-bridge.git
./start.command        # macOS: serves http://localhost:8787 and opens Chrome; or: node serve.mjs
```

Reload the page once after updating (the old page is cached with the old version stamp).

## Known limits (please read)

- **No login, by design.** On a Mac the server listens on `localhost` only; in Home Assistant it is open to your LAN, like the machine's own API. Do not forward its ports to the internet.
- **The Portal driver is untested on a physical Portal by the author.**
- **EC is not TDS**; TDS/EY are estimates from your own refractometer readings.
- **The AI review reads numbers**, not the cup.
- **Unsigned macOS app; Home Assistant builds on the device.**
- The security review was done **by the same AI assistant that wrote the code**, reading every file and testing each fix; it is not an independent audit. Reports are welcome — *Security → Report a vulnerability*.
