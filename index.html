        Refractometer reading for this shot

        Calibration for this coffee

        EC tracks ions, a refractometer tracks everything dissolved; the ratio between them is specific to a coffee, a water and a Portal. Five or more paired shots make the estimate trustworthy inside the measured range.

        Send to Beanconqueror

        Scan with the phone that has Beanconqueror installed. The QR carries a thinned trace; the full-resolution link is below for AirDrop or Messages.

        Beanconqueror has no field for an imported TDS, so the EC analysis and the estimate land in the brew note and as a custom EC trace; type the refractometer TDS into Beanconqueror's own field if you want its EY.
